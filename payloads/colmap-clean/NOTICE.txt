scAnt payload colmap-clean 4.1.1-1 (Windows x64) - third-party notices
=========================================================================

This archive holds COLMAP 4.1.1 (https://github.com/colmap/colmap, tag 4.1.1,
commit a0d785fba74b2664f31edc4a29026a8b27c00f67), built from its sources for
scAnt without COLMAP's GPL, AGPL and non-commercial parts. The recipe is in
payloads/colmap-clean/build/ of https://github.com/FabianPlum/scAnt-payloads.
Build of record: 2026-10-17, vcpkg a0b1c8d3a477c1cb4813d8e127a56961707ca42b,
MSVC 14.44.35207, CUDA 12.8. manifest.json lists every file and its sha256;
licence-proof.json holds the import tables that show what each binary links.

Every component is named below with its licence. The full licence texts are
in licences/<name>.txt (the name in brackets).

4. In licences/ without a library of their own in bin/
-----------------------------------------------------
The build carries the licence of every port its vcpkg installed. No binary
here imports a library of these ports (licence-proof.json); where one is
header-only or static, its code may be compiled into the libraries above
(fmt into OpenImageIO): fmt [fmt], GLEW [glew], OpenGL [opengl], the OpenGL
and EGL registries (headers) [opengl-registry, egl-registry], JasPer
[jasper], and vcpkg's helper ports, which build nothing [vcpkg-boost,
vcpkg-cmake, vcpkg-cmake-config].
